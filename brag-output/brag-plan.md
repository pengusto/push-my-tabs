# Brag Plan: Push My Tabs

## Nine inspection answers
1. A local Chrome/Firefox extension for direction-based tab switching, moving and last-used cycling.
2. Its useful claim: switch and move tabs the way they appear.
3. Existing purple Push P and compact five-view dark sidebar.
4. The real Navigation, TabLoop, Layout, Shortcut bindings and Settings views.
5. A 21.5-second UI walkthrough gives each of the five areas a readable beat.
6. App-store tone: restrained, precise and readable without narration.
7. A quiet rhythmic bed and one restrained logo accent; no voiceover.
8. “Deine Tabs. Im Fluss. Mit Push My Tabs wechselst und verschiebst du Tabs per Richtungstaste — und springst mit TabLoop zurück.”
9. The UI flow: open the navigation view, inspect TabLoop, choose the layout, see native key formatting and adjust local preferences.

## Format and identity
1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 21.5 seconds. Background #14131b, text #f4f2fa, accent #a394ff, original #8167ff logo. Inter for film copy; the UI keeps its actual system font. No replacement logo or decorative effects.
